How is my score calculated?

Score = max(0, 100 − risk points). Each critical finding adds 20 risk points, high 8, medium 3, low 1, and informational 0. This report has {{ 100 - score }}{% if score == 0 %} or more{% endif %} weighted risk points.

The score uses finding volume, not inventory coverage or a compliance standard. An empty or incomplete report can score 100. The critical-free streak counts distinct loaded snapshots in timestamp order; re-uploading the same normalized report does not extend it.
